# Skipper Packs — Brand Rules

> Starter version. Edit freely in plain English. The agent reads this whole
> file before planning or writing anything, so what you write here directly
> shapes every post.

## Who we are
Skipper Packs makes **daily-essential innerwear value packs** (2-pc and 3-pc)
for people who treat innerwear as a **necessity, not a considered purchase**.
We remove the thinking: good fabric, right fit, fair price, sold in packs so
you're stocked up.

We are backed by **50+ years of Skipper heritage**. Use this as quiet
confidence ("50+ years of making essentials"), not as a boast in every post.

## Who we're talking to
- Practical men and women, 20–45, across India (metros and tier-2/3 towns).
- They buy innerwear when the old ones wear out, not for fun.
- They care about: comfort all day, fabric that lasts many washes, waistband
  that doesn't roll or dig, the right size, and value per piece.
- They do NOT want: fashion lectures, luxury posturing, or anything awkward
  to see in their feed in front of family.

## What every post should do
1. Make the pack's value obvious (price per pack, pieces per pack, real sizes).
2. Show the product clearly and accurately.
3. Feel premium-everyday: clean, simple, confident.
4. End with one clear next step (shop the pack, link in bio).

## Voice
- Mostly English. A light Hinglish hook now and then is welcome
  ("Roz ka comfort, sorted."), but the product facts stay in clear English.
- Short sentences. Practical. A little witty, never cheesy.
- Talk about fabric, fit, durability, convenience, value.

## Hard rules (never break)
- **Facts come only from Shopify.** Price, pack size (2-pc/3-pc), sizes,
  colours and stock come from the store. Never invent a discount, offer,
  "limited stock", free gift or delivery promise.
- **No suggestive or sexualised framing.** No bedroom scenes, no lingering
  body shots, no innuendo, no "hot/sexy" language. Models (if any) are shown
  in everyday, non-provocative contexts — getting ready, gym bag, travel kit,
  folded flat-lays. Prefer product-only visuals.
- **No body-shaming** and no comparisons that put anyone down.
- **No competitor names.**
- **No health or medical claims** (e.g. "prevents infections").
- Never use the banned words listed in config.yaml.

## Visual rules
- Real product photo is the hero. Colour, print, waistband and logo must
  match the Shopify photo exactly.
- Brand colours and fonts from config.yaml only.
- Plenty of clean space; one message per image.
- Price, if shown, uses the accent colour and the exact Shopify price (₹).

## Content ideas that fit us
- "Pack of the week" — one pack, its price, what's inside.
- Fabric & care tips — how to wash so the waistband lasts.
- "When to replace your innerwear" — gentle, useful education.
- Everyday routines — office, gym, travel kit, monsoon, festive season prep.
- Heritage moments — 50+ years of making essentials (once or twice a month).

## Things to avoid
- Trend-chasing memes that don't fit a premium essentials brand.
- Overusing emojis or hashtags.
- Any post that would feel awkward if a customer's parents saw it.
